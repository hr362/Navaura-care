Inclusion & Awareness

NavAura Care is all about inclusion. Everyone deserves the chance to live their life, in the way they can.

Not every disability is visible

We understand all kinds of disability. Some you can see. Many you can't, like autism, mental health conditions, acquired brain injury, chronic pain or intellectual disability.

We listen, we believe you, and we never judge. Your support is based on what you tell us you need.

What we believe

  • Choice and controlYou decide how, when and by whom you are supported.
  • Being heardYour voice leads every plan and every conversation.
  • Dignity in personal careCare given with privacy, patience and respect.
  • Treating everyone equallyWhatever your background, culture, faith, identity or ability.
  • Opportunity for everyoneTo move, communicate, connect and participate.
  • Families includedWe work alongside the people who matter to you.

Person-centred, always

Support should fit the person, not the other way around.

We welcome people of every background, including LGBTQIA+ communities, Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, and culturally diverse families.

We are here to help

Accepting new referrals now. We reply to every referral within 24 hours.
