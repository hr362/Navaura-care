Understanding the NDIS

A plain-language guide for participants, families and carers.

What is the NDIS?

The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) funds reasonable and necessary supports for Australians with a permanent and significant disability. Each participant has an NDIS plan that sets out their goals and the funding available to pursue them.

Your plan budgets

  • Core supportsEveryday assistance, such as personal care, household tasks, community participation and transport.
  • Capacity Building supportsSupports that build independence and skills over time, such as life skills development, support coordination and employment support.
  • Capital supportsAssistive technology, home modifications and Specialist Disability Accommodation.

How your plan can be managed

  • NDIA-managedThe NDIA pays providers directly. You can only use registered providers, such as NavAura Care.
  • Plan-managedA plan manager pays providers on your behalf.
  • Self-managedYou manage the funding and pay providers yourself.

NavAura Care works with participants under all three plan management types.

Why choose a registered provider?

Registered providers are approved by the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission and are audited against the NDIS Practice Standards. NavAura Care Pty Ltd is a registered NDIS provider (registration 4-MBZ2CY4). All our workers hold an NDIS Worker Screening Check and follow the NDIS Code of Conduct.

Service agreements and pricing

Before services begin, we agree a written service agreement setting out the supports, schedule, costs and how changes or cancellations are handled. Our prices follow the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits.

Changing providers

If you would like to move to NavAura Care, we can help you review your current arrangements and plan a smooth transition, with no gap in your supports.
