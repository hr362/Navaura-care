Work With NavAura Care

We are looking for kind, reliable support workers who are passionate about helping people live with choice, dignity and independence.

Who we are looking for

  • Compassionate, patient and respectful
  • Experienced in disability support, community access, mental health, behaviour support, high intensity or complex needs
  • Reliable, professional and able to work independently
  • Supportive of multicultural communities and diversity
  • Strong communicators who want to help others

Documents we need

  • NDIS Worker Screening Check
  • Police Check
  • First Aid and CPR Certificate
  • Driver's Licence (for roles that include transport)
  • Relevant qualifications, e.g. Individual Support, Community Services or Mental Health
