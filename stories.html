Stories of Support

Behind every plan is a person and a family. These are some of the journeys we've been part of.

Autism · Family support

A family who no longer felt alone

A family caring for their child with autism was exhausted and unsure where to turn. Every day felt like a struggle, and the whole family was feeling it.

We built support around the child's routines and the way they communicate, and worked alongside the parents rather than taking over. Over time, the child grew more settled and confident, and the family finally had space to breathe and be a family again.

Living alone

Someone in their corner

Some of the people we support live alone, without family nearby and without financial support to fall back on. Day-to-day life can feel overwhelming and isolating.

We helped with the basics first, like routines, meals, appointments and keeping the home running, and then connected them with community services and supports they didn't know they could access. Most of all, they had someone reliable in their corner.

Confidence · Study · Work

From feeling left out to flying colours

A young person we supported felt left out and didn't believe they belonged in the community. Their confidence was low, and their studies and career had stalled.

Step by step, we supported them to get out, try new things and stand up for themselves. They got their studies and career back on track and passed with flying colours. Most importantly, they now feel respected, confident and worthy of the life they want.

Every person is unique. Every journey is different.
