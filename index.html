Who we are

NavAura Care is a registered NDIS support provider committed to high-quality, person-centred support. We understand that every disability is different, and not every disability is visible.

Our team brings qualifications in individual support, nursing and social work, and a genuine commitment to the people we support.

Our promise

"Every person deserves support that respects who they are and what they want from life."

NavAura Care was founded to deliver the consistent, respectful support every participant and family deserves. Our team brings experience across disability support, high and complex needs, mental health, children and young people, and support coordination.

All services

Support in your language

Our workers speak English, Hindi, Punjabi, Telugu, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese and african languages. We match you with someone who understands your culture, faith, food and family.

We cover all of Sydney, with most of our team in Western and South West Sydney.

Getting started

  1. Refer or call

    Send a referral, email or phone. We reply within 24 hours.

  2. Initial meeting

    We arrange a meet-and-greet with you and your proposed support worker, at home or in the community, so you can be confident in the match.

  3. Service agreement

    We agree your supports, roster and costs in writing, complete any risk assessments, and begin services.
