Making an NDIS Referral to NavAura Care: What Happens Next

What happens after you make an NDIS referral to NavAura Care, from our 24-hour reply to the meet-and-greet, service agreement and first shift.

By the NavAura Care team · 15 November 2026

Key points
  • We reply to every referral within 24 hours.
  • We arrange a meet-and-greet with you and your proposed support worker.
  • We agree supports, rosters and costs in a written service agreement before starting.
  • Participants, families, support coordinators and other professionals can all refer.

Making a referral is simple, and it doesn't commit you to anything. Here is what happens after you contact us.

1. You send a referral or call us

Use our online referral form, call 0431 404 000, email services@navauracare.com.au or message us on WhatsApp. Participants, family members, carers, support coordinators, plan managers and hospital staff can all refer.

2. We reply within 24 hours

We contact you to talk about the support you are looking for, your goals, your preferred days and times, and any preferences for your support worker, such as language, culture or gender.

3. Meet-and-greet

We arrange a meet-and-greet with you, your family and your proposed support worker, at home or in the community, so everyone is confident in the match before services begin.

4. Service agreement and support plan

We agree the supports, roster and costs in a clear written service agreement in line with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits. We complete any risk assessments and a support plan.

5. Support begins

Your support starts with consistent, familiar workers. We check in regularly and keep you, your family and your support coordinator informed.

What to have handy

  • The participant's name, suburb and contact details
  • The supports you are looking for
  • Your NDIS number and plan dates, if you have them
  • How your plan is managed

Please email reports or plans to services@navauracare.com.au rather than including them in the online form.

This article is general information, not advice about your individual plan. For official information visit ndis.gov.au.
