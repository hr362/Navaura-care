Frequently Asked Questions

Can't find your answer? Call 0431 404 000.

Are you a registered NDIS provider?

Yes. NavAura Care Pty Ltd is a registered NDIS provider (registration 4-MBZ2CY4). You can check us on the NDIS Commission's provider register.

What is the NDIS?

The National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS) funds support for Australians with a permanent and significant disability. If you have an NDIS plan, you can use it to pay for supports like ours.

Do you support NDIA-managed, plan-managed and self-managed participants?

Yes. As a registered provider we can support all three.

How much does support cost?

Our prices follow the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits. We explain all costs in your service agreement before support starts.

How quickly will you reply?

We reply to every referral within 24 hours.

Can I choose my support worker?

Yes. We match you based on your needs, language, culture, gender preference and interests, and you can ask for a change at any time.

Do you support children?

Yes, including children with autism. We provide everyday support at home and in the community, and can refer you to allied health for therapy. Read more.

Do you support people with high or complex support needs?

Yes. We are registered for High Intensity Daily Personal Activities and implement behaviour support plans. Read more.

Do you support people living with psychosocial disability?

Yes. We provide non-clinical, recovery-focused disability support for participants living with psychosocial disability. We don’t provide clinical mental health treatment. Read more.

How do you choose my support worker?

We match each participant with a support worker based on their needs, goals, preferences, language and culture. A meet-and-greet takes place before services begin, and you can request a change of worker at any time.

Do you support people whose disability isn't visible?

Yes. Many disabilities, like autism and mental health conditions, can't be seen. We listen, believe you and support you on your terms. Read more.

Which areas do you cover?

All of Sydney, with most of our team in Western and South West Sydney. North Sydney on request. See all areas.

Do you provide support coordination?

Yes. We provide Support Coordination Level 1 (Support Connection) and Level 2 (Coordination of Supports). Read more.

Do you provide therapy?

No. We do not provide allied health or therapy, but we can suggest allied health providers, such as occupational therapists, physiotherapists, speech pathologists and psychologists, or help with a referral. Read more.

Do you have SDA or accommodation available?

Not at the moment, but we may have properties available soon. Contact us or make a referral and we’ll work on it together. Read more.

Can you provide transport?

Yes. We have our own van for appointments, outings and activities. Read more.

How do I make a complaint?

Call or email us, or contact the NDIS Commission. See how.
