# WhatsApp setup for NavAura Care (0431 404 000)

The website has a WhatsApp chat button on every page (`assets/whatsapp.js` and `assets/whatsapp.css`). Visitors pick an option or type a message, and WhatsApp opens with the message ready to send to **+61 431 404 000**. To change the number, options or wording, edit the `NUMBER` and `OPTIONS` at the top of `assets/whatsapp.js`.

For automatic replies inside WhatsApp, use the free **WhatsApp Business** app on the phone that has this number. Open WhatsApp Business, go to **Settings → Business tools**, and set up the following.

## 4. Quick replies (Business tools → Quick replies → +)
Type `/` in a chat to use them.

| Shortcut | Message |
|---|---|
| `/referral` | Thanks! You can complete our referral form here (3 minutes): https://www.navauracare.com.au/referral.html, or reply with the participant's name, suburb, NDIS number (if known), and the support needed. |
| `/services` | We provide personal care, high intensity support, SIL, community participation, life skills, psychosocial support, children's support, accessible transport and support coordination. Full list: https://www.navauracare.com.au/services.html |
| `/availability` | Could you share the suburb, the days and times you need support, and any worker preferences (gender, language)? We'll confirm availability within 24 hours. |
| `/meet` | Before services begin we arrange a meet-and-greet with you and your proposed support worker, at home or in the community. What day and time suits you? |
| `/ndis` | New to the NDIS? This page explains the basics and how plans are managed: https://www.navauracare.com.au/ndis-info.html |
| `/jobs` | Thanks for your interest in working with us! Apply here: https://www.navauracare.com.au/work-with-us.html, then email your resume and certificates to services@navauracare.com.au |
| `/reports` | For privacy, please email reports, plans or documents to services@navauracare.com.au rather than sending them on WhatsApp. |
| `/thanks` | Thank you for contacting NavAura Care. Please reach out any time on 0431 404 000. |

## 5. Labels (Chats → ⋮ → Labels)
Create labels to track enquiries: **New referral**, **Coordinator**, **Waiting on info**, **Booked meet & greet**, **Job applicant**.

## 6. Catalogue (optional)
Business tools → Catalogue: add one item per service with a short description and link to its page on the website.
