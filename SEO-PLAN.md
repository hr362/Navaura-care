# NavAura Care: SEO and AI visibility plan

Goal: appear near the top of Google (including the map pack) and get named by AI assistants (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude, Perplexity, Copilot) when someone asks for an NDIS provider in Western Sydney, especially Liverpool, Blacktown, Merrylands, Bankstown, Fairfield and Parramatta.

No one can guarantee a first-page ranking or an AI recommendation. What reliably moves the needle for a local NDIS provider is: a complete Google Business Profile, steady genuine reviews, consistent listings across directories, suburb and service pages that answer real questions, and fresh helpful content. This plan covers all five.

---

## 1. What the competition does (research, November 2026)

Searching "NDIS provider Liverpool / Blacktown / Bankstown / Merrylands / Parramatta" shows:

- **Directories dominate page one**: Carevo, DisabilityProviders.com, Leap in!, MyCareSpace, mdhomecare and the NDIS Commission register. Being listed on these is the fastest way onto page one and into AI answers.
- **Bigger providers use suburb landing pages**, for example Ability Options has separate Liverpool, Merrylands and Parramatta pages, and others use pages like "NDIS provider in Blacktown", "SIL Blacktown" and "NDIS support coordination in Parramatta".
- **AI assistants lean on Google Business Profile, reviews and directory data.** Industry studies suggest Gemini relies most on Google Maps data, and that ChatGPT and Perplexity names often come from directories and review sites. Contact details that don't match across sites reduce your chances.

We have now matched the suburb-page approach (section 3). The biggest remaining gaps are off-site: Google Business Profile, reviews and directory listings (sections 4 to 6).

## 3. Done on the website (November 2026)

- **Six suburb pages**: Liverpool, Blacktown, Merrylands, Bankstown, Fairfield, Parramatta, each with its own text, nearby suburbs, services, FAQs and structured data, plus an **Areas We Serve** page.
- **Blog with six guides**: choosing a provider, SIL, support coordination levels, community participation ideas, plan management, referral process.
- **Structured data (schema)** that Google and AI tools read: LocalBusiness with services, areas, languages and social profiles on the home page; Service, FAQPage and BreadcrumbList on suburb pages; Article on blog posts; FAQPage on the FAQ page.
- **Keyword-rich page titles** on the home page and all service pages.
- **AI access**: robots.txt explicitly allows ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini and Apple AI crawlers; **llms.txt** gives AI tools a plain summary of who NavAura Care is, services, areas and contact details.
- **Sitemap** updated with all 40 pages. Internal links from the home page and footer to every suburb page and the blog.

**Your next step:** in Google Search Console, resubmit `sitemap.xml` and use URL Inspection → Request indexing on the home page and the six suburb pages.

## 4. Google Business Profile (most important single step)

1. Claim or create the profile at business.google.com as a **service-area business** (hide the street address if you don't see clients there).
2. Primary category: **Disability services and support organisation**. Secondary: **Home health care service**, **Social services organisation** (pick the closest available).
3. Service areas: Liverpool, Blacktown, Merrylands, Bankstown, Fairfield, Parramatta, Cabramatta, Campbelltown, Penrith (up to 20).
4. Add every service with a short description, using the keyword phrases above.
5. Business name exactly **NavAura Care** (don't add keywords to the name; Google penalises this).
6. Phone 0431 404 000, website https://www.navauracare.com.au, hours "Open 24 hours".
7. Upload 15+ real photos (team in uniform, van, activities, with consent).
8. Post weekly (a new blog post, an activity, a hiring update).
9. Turn on messaging and add the Q&A from the FAQ page.
10. Do the same on **Bing Places** (bingplaces.com, used by ChatGPT and Copilot) and **Apple Business Connect** (Siri and Apple Maps).

## 5. Reviews

- Ask happy participants, families and support coordinators for a Google review. A short text or WhatsApp message with your review link works best.
- Aim for 2 to 4 new reviews a month, steadily. Reply to every review within a few days.
- Never offer gifts for reviews or write fake ones (against Google policy and Australian Consumer Law).
- Gemini and Google Maps use reviews directly; reviews that mention a suburb and service ("great SIL support in Blacktown") help most.
