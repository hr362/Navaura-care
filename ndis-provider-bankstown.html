Disability support in Bankstown

NavAura Care is a registered NDIS provider supporting participants in Bankstown and across the Canterbury-Bankstown area. We deliver personal care, community participation, Supported Independent Living and support coordination.

We take time to understand what matters to you, then roster consistent workers who respect your culture, faith, food and family, with a meet-and-greet before services begin.

Support can include shopping and errands in Bankstown, getting to appointments and programs, and taking part in local arts, sport and community events.

Our NDIS services in Bankstown

  • Personal care and daily living – Showering, dressing, meals, medication prompts and household routines, including overnight support.
  • Supported Independent Living (SIL) – Drop-in to 24/7 support in a shared or individual home.
  • High intensity support – Complex bowel care, PEG feeding, catheter care and epilepsy management by trained workers.
  • Community participation – Outings, activities, sport, hobbies and groups to connect with your community.
  • Early childhood supports – Everyday support for children and families, mentoring and help around school.
  • Accessible transport – Our wheelchair-accessible NavAura Care van with a support worker.
  • Support coordination – Level 1 Support Connection and Level 2 Coordination of Supports.
  • Psychosocial support – Non-clinical, recovery-focused support for participants living with psychosocial disability.
  • Accommodation/tenancy support – Help finding accommodation and maintaining a tenancy. No SDA properties available right now.

We don’t provide allied health or therapy, but we can suggest providers in your area or help with a referral. How referrals work.

Getting started in Bankstown

  1. Refer or call

    Send a referral, call 0431 404 000 or message us on WhatsApp. We reply within 24 hours.

  2. Meet-and-greet

    We meet you and your proposed support worker at home or in the community in Bankstown, so you can be confident in the match.

  3. Service agreement

    We agree your supports, roster and costs in writing, in line with the NDIS Pricing Arrangements and Price Limits, then begin.

Questions about NDIS support in Bankstown

Is NavAura Care a registered NDIS provider in Bankstown?

Yes. NavAura Care Pty Ltd is a registered NDIS provider (registration 4-MBZ2CY4) and we support participants in Bankstown and the surrounding suburbs.

Which suburbs near Bankstown do you cover?

We support participants in Bankstown and nearby suburbs including Yagoona, Punchbowl, Revesby, Padstow, Condell Park, Bass Hill, Greenacre, Chester Hill, plus the rest of Western and South West Sydney. See all areas.

How quickly can support start in Bankstown?

We reply to every referral within 24 hours. After a meet-and-greet and a service agreement, we start as soon as the right worker is available.

Can I choose a worker who speaks my language?

Yes. Our team speaks English, Hindi, Punjabi, Telugu, Arabic, Greek, Vietnamese and African languages, and you can tell us your preferences, including worker gender.

Do you have SDA or accommodation available in Bankstown?

Not at the moment, but we may have properties available soon. Make a referral and we’ll work on it together. Read more.
