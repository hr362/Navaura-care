We do not provide plan management or therapy in-house. We work closely with the participant's existing practitioners, and have partner links with physiotherapists, exercise physiologists, speech pathologists, behaviour support practitioners and social workers.

What you can expect

  • Fast intakeReply within 24 hours, then a meet-and-greet with the participant.
  • The right matchWorkers matched on needs, gender preference, language and culture.
  • High intensity supportHigh intensity daily personal activities, 2:1 support and implementation of behaviour support plans.
  • Clear reportingProgress updates and reports when you need them for plan reviews.
  • NDIS pricingRates within the NDIS Price Limits, with cancellations handled under the NDIS Pricing Arrangements.
