High Intensity Daily Personal Activities

Skilled support for participants whose daily personal activities involve complex health or support needs.

Make A ReferralCall 0431 404 000

About this support

High intensity supports are delivered by support workers who are trained and assessed as competent for the specific support each participant requires. We complete a thorough risk assessment before services begin and work closely with the participant's health professionals.

Where the participant's needs and plan require it, we provide 2:1 support with consistent, familiar pairs of workers. We also implement behaviour support plans developed by the participant's NDIS behaviour support practitioner.

What this support can include

  • Complex bowel care
  • Enteral (PEG) feeding and management
  • Urinary catheter management
  • Epilepsy and seizure management
  • Complex swallowing and mealtime support
  • Pressure care and specialised mobility support
  • 2:1 support
  • Implementation of positive behaviour support plans

We do not develop behaviour support plans. We implement plans written by the participant's NDIS behaviour support practitioner and report as required.

Who this support is for

  • Participants with complex health needs requiring high intensity support
  • Participants who require two workers for safety
  • Participants with a behaviour support plan in place

How we begin

  1. Referral or enquiry

    Contact us by phone, email or our referral form. We respond within 24 hours.

  2. Initial meeting

    We arrange a meet-and-greet with the participant, their family and the proposed support worker, so everyone is confident in the match before services begin.

  3. Service agreement and support plan

    We agree the supports, roster and costs in a clear service agreement, complete any risk assessments, and begin services.
