Development of Daily Living and Life Skills

Practical, goal-focused support to build the skills participants need for greater independence.

Make A ReferralCall 0431 404 000

About this support

Life skills supports focus on building capacity rather than doing things for the participant. Together we set clear goals and practise skills in real settings, at home and in the community, until the participant feels confident.

Progress is reviewed regularly and shared with the participant and, with their consent, their family and support coordinator to support plan reviews.

What this support can include

  • Cooking, meal planning and nutrition
  • Budgeting and managing money
  • Using public transport safely
  • Household management and personal organisation
  • Communication and social skills
  • Preparing for independent living

  • Young people moving into adulthood
  • Participants preparing to live more independently
  • Participants with capacity building goals in their NDIS plan

How we begin

  1. Referral or enquiry

    Contact us by phone, email or our referral form. We respond within 24 hours.

  2. Initial meeting

    We arrange a meet-and-greet with the participant, their family and the proposed support worker, so everyone is confident in the match before services begin.

  3. Service agreement and support plan

    We agree the supports, roster and costs in a clear service agreement, complete any risk assessments, and begin services.
